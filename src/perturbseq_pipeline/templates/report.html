3. Perturbation strength

For every target gene that is also measured in the expression matrix, the gene's own expression is compared between cells carrying guides against it and control cells. An effective CRISPR perturbation lowers that expression, so a target is only called effective when the reduction is significant (KS test, BH-FDR < {{ cfg.perturbation.fdr_alpha }}) and the fold change is negative.
